03 — Projects

Featured 2025 — Present · team of 2

Accessible Academic — AI Lecture Platform

Turns university lecture recordings into captioned video and generated study material — topics, summaries and flashcards — built for students who cannot rely on listening alone. An asynchronous FastAPI pipeline moves each recording through FFmpeg normalization, AssemblyAI Hebrew speech-to-text with word-level timestamps, and LLM generation, so long jobs run in the background while the API stays responsive.

  • ↳ Grounding validation pass re-checks every summary and flashcard against the source transcript, rewriting or dropping unsupported claims.
  • ↳ Asynchronous pipeline orchestrating ingestion, transcription and generation without blocking the API.
  • ↳ Serverless on Vercel over PostgreSQL, with full RTL/Hebrew support including PDF export.
Python FastAPI PostgreSQL SQLModel FFmpeg AssemblyAI OpenAI Vercel

Featured 2026 — Present

BiteWise — Nutrition Tracking PWA

A bilingual (Hebrew/English) nutrition-tracking app running in production, with full RTL/LTR layout, Google sign-in and PWA install. Scans barcodes live against Open Food Facts, and for private-label products that exist in no database at all, reads the nutrition label straight off a photo.

  • ↳ Live barcode scanning — native BarcodeDetector with a ZXing fallback — resolved against Open Food Facts.
  • ↳ Every table secured with per-user Row Level Security; AI calls rate-limited per user and cached in Postgres to keep cost bounded.
  • ↳ Calorie and macro targets from Mifflin–St Jeor; workout expenditure from MET and ACSM equations.
